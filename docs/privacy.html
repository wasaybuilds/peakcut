Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

PeakCut is free software that you install and run on your own computer, and its source code is public. It has no PeakCut server and no PeakCut account. The developers of PeakCut never receive your data, your videos or your account access.

Google and YouTube data

When you click Connect YouTube, you sign in with Google and grant PeakCut two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): used only to upload the clips you choose to post, with the title, description, tags and privacy setting you see before posting.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): used only to show the name of the connected channel in PeakCut's settings.

The sign-in token Google returns is saved in a file on your computer and is used only to make those requests to the YouTube Data API from your computer. PeakCut does not read, store or analyse any other data from your Google or YouTube account.

PeakCut does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine learning models.

PeakCut's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

PeakCut uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Instagram data

When you connect Instagram, you paste an access token from your own Meta developer app. PeakCut saves it in a file on your computer and uses it only to read your Instagram username and to publish the Reels you choose to post, with the caption you approve. It is not shared with anyone.

Videos you clip

To find moments in a video, PeakCut downloads the public YouTube video you paste, then sends its audio or transcript to the speech-to-text and AI services you set up with your own API keys (for example Groq or Z.ai). This is the content of the video you are clipping, not data from your Google, YouTube or Instagram account. Those services handle it under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Everything PeakCut stores (downloads, clips, its database and your account tokens) lives in its folder on your computer. Deleting that folder deletes all of it.
  • Clicking Disconnect in PeakCut's settings deletes the saved token for that account.
  • You can remove PeakCut's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can remove access to Instagram in Instagram's settings under Website permissions, then Apps and websites.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be asked by opening an issue on PeakCut's GitHub page.
